Privacy at Shortext

Privacy Policy

Shortext is designed to keep your shortcuts and saved text on your device. This policy explains what the desktop application processes and what happens when you visit our website.

Last updated September 12, 2026

Overview

Shortext is a local-first Windows text-expansion application developed by ROXI. The desktop application does not require an account and does not send your snippets, groups, typed text, or clipboard contents to ROXI.

Desktop data

The Shortext desktop application processes and stores the following information locally:

  • Snippets and groups: names, shortcuts, expanded text, and group organization you create.
  • Backups: automatic daily backups and any manual backups you create.
  • Settings: application preferences needed for Shortext to operate.

This information is stored in the Shortext folder inside your Windows roaming application-data directory, normally %AppData%\Shortext. Import and export files are saved only in locations you choose.

Keyboard input

While expansion is active, Shortext observes keyboard input system-wide and temporarily processes recent characters in memory to detect a configured shortcut. Shortext does not create a history of everything you type, and the detected input is not transmitted to ROXI or any third party by the application.

You can pause global text expansion at any time from the application or its system-tray control.

Clipboard use

To insert saved text reliably into another application, Shortext temporarily places the selected snippet content on the Windows clipboard and attempts to restore the previous clipboard value after expansion. Clipboard contents are processed locally and are not sent to ROXI.

Collection and sharing

The Shortext desktop application contains no ROXI-operated analytics, advertising, account system, or cloud synchronization. ROXI does not sell personal information or share desktop-app data for advertising.

If you download Shortext through a third-party marketplace, that marketplace may process download, account, payment, or diagnostic information under its own privacy policy.

Website data

The Shortext website does not provide user accounts, advertising, or ROXI-operated analytics and does not intentionally set tracking cookies. The website is hosted by Vercel, which may process standard technical request information—such as an IP address, browser type, requested URL, and timestamps—to deliver and protect the service under Vercel's own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Desktop data remains on your device until you delete it. Shortext retains up to 30 automatic daily backups; manual backups remain until you remove them. You can delete individual snippets and groups in Shortext or remove all locally stored data by deleting %AppData%\Shortext after closing the application.

Security

Shortext limits desktop processing to the local device. You are responsible for securing your Windows account, exported files, and any sensitive information you choose to save as a snippet. Avoid placing passwords, payment-card data, or other highly sensitive secrets in snippets.

Children's privacy

Shortext is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. ROXI does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the desktop application.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when Shortext's features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact ROXI through the Shortext support page.
